18 #7

Ni mangangalunya.

English: In Bible Journal #3, we spoke about Ten Commandments Are Still in Full Effect, and here, we spoke about what Jesus has added in the ten commandments. In Bible Journal #4 we spoke of the Thou Shalt Not Kill. In this day and age, is where a lot of problem persist. The days of 1920s - 1950s is my favorite, as pornography is not rampant, despite prostitution was a thing arleady, since the day of Romans in before Jesus times. But with social media, and all, we are comitting a sin, that Jesus has explicitly told on Mateo 5:27-28 and a written instruction of what to do with it that can be read both on Mateo 5:29-30 and Marcos 9:43-48, that if we are looking at someone with lust, we are already committing adultery. And this is the new law Jesus added to the #7 Neither shalt thou commit adultery. This is a very serious sin, and it is a sin that can lead to other sins, such as fornication, and even more than physically comitting it. A stare has already made it a sin.

Tagalog: Sa Bible Journal #3, tinalakay natin na ang Sampung Utos ay nananatiling may bisa, at dito naman, tatalakayin natin ang mga idinagdag ni Hesus sa mga utos na ito. Sa Bible Journal #4, tinalakay natin ang tungkol sa "Huwag kang papatay." Sa panahon ngayon, maraming problema ang lumalabas dahil dito. Ang mga dekada ng 1920s hanggang 1950s ang aking paborito, dahil hindi pa laganap ang pornograpiya, kahit na ang prostitusyon ay isang bagay na umiiral na mula pa noong panahon ng mga Romano bago si Hesus. Ngunit sa social media at iba pang mga platform, tayo ay nagkakasala sa isang paraan na tahasang sinabi ni Hesus sa Mateo 5:27-28, at may nakasulat na instruksyon kung paano harapin ito sa Mateo 5:29-30 at Marcos 9:43-48, na kung tayo ay tumitingin sa isang tao nang may pagnanasa, tayo ay nagkakasala ng pangangalunya. At ito ang bagong kautusang idinagdag ni Hesus sa #7 Ni mangangalunya. Ito ay isang napakaseryosong kasalanan, at maaaring magdulot ito ng iba pang mga kasalanan tulad ng fornication, at higit pa kaysa sa pisikal na pagkomit nito. Ang isang tingin lamang ay nagiging kasalanan na.

English: Jesus meant, symbolically stopping what our body parts have done wrong, than our entire body be burnt in hell. And he meant it, as people might read on Book of Enoch, that was included in old times, that was then removed in new times, were fallen angels are reprimanded by God, and is going to pay their sins. If fallen angels will face such judgement, we will be too. As you can read below on Mark 9:43-48 it was again spoken as the verse you have read above from Matthew.

Kung ang mga nahulog na anghel ay haharap sa ganitong paghuhukom, tayo rin ay haharap dito. Tulad ng mababasa mo sa ibaba sa Markos 9:43-48, ito ay muling sinabi bilang berso na nabasa mo sa itaas mula kay Mateo.
